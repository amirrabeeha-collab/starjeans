PROJECT OVERVIEW

A polished online home for a local fashion brand.

Star Jeans 819 needed a simple, modern digital storefront that could put its clothing collection first while making it easy for visitors to browse, discover categories, and get in touch.

Role Web Design & Development
Industry Fashion & Retail
Focus Product Discovery
Experience Mobile-first
01

The Challenge

The brand needed a stronger online presence where customers could quickly understand what it sells instead of relying only on social channels or in-person browsing.

02

The Approach

We shaped the experience around strong fashion imagery, clear navigation, category-led browsing, and a straightforward contact path.

03

The Result

A clean storefront concept that gives Star Jeans 819 a recognizable digital destination for its jeans, T-shirts, hoodies, and future collections.
